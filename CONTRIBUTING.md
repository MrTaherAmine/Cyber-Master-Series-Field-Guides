# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve **Cyber Master Series Field Guides**.

## Contribution principles

Contributions should be:
- original or properly attributed;
- technically accurate;
- practical and readable;
- grounded in authoritative sources where possible;
- respectful of legal, ethical, privacy and safety boundaries;
- vendor-neutral unless a guide is explicitly vendor-specific.

## Preferred contribution types

- corrections and source updates;
- practitioner examples;
- checklists and templates;
- diagrams;
- labs designed for authorized environments;
- cross-reference improvements;
- accessibility and translation improvements.

## Content quality

Avoid:
- AI-generated filler;
- copied standards text;
- unverified claims;
- unsafe operational instructions without appropriate context;
- marketing disguised as guidance.

## Attribution

Do not remove the project copyright and maintainer attribution.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the repository's applicable content or code license.
