## What this repository is

**Cyber Master Series Field Guides** is an open practitioner body of knowledge for the entire information-security ecosystem.

It is intentionally broader than a certification guide, a blue-team handbook, or an offensive-security collection. The long-term scope spans:

| Strategic | Governance | Engineering | Defense | Deep Technical | Specialized |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CISO & executive leadership | GRC, risk, audit & assurance | IAM, crypto, network, cloud, AppSec | SOC, CSIRT, IR, CTI, hunting | DFIR, malware, RE, vuln research | Privacy, OT/IACS, IoT, cars, satellites, telecom, AI & more |

The repository is designed to grow **from leadership and governance down through architecture, engineering, operations, investigation, offensive security, low-level research, and specialized cyber-physical domains**.

> **The goal is not to publish everything at once. The goal is to maintain one coherent structure where everything has a home, and then build each guide properly, step by step.**

## 🧱 Repository design

Every domain follows:

```text
NN-domain/
├── README.md
├── field-guides/
│   ├── 01-specific-field-guide/
│   │   ├── README.md
│   │   ├── 00-overview/
│   │   ├── 01-core-concepts/
│   │   ├── 02-practical-guidance/
│   │   ├── 03-governance-risk-assurance/
│   │   ├── 04-checklists-and-templates/
│   │   ├── 05-labs-exercises-and-case-studies/
│   │   └── 06-references/
│   └── ...
└── resources/
```

This gives every future topic a predictable home while keeping the top-level repository readable.
